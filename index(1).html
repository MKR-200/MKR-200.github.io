Econometrics · Electricity markets

Battery Build-out and the Shape of California Electricity Prices

A conditional-association study of whether California's rapid expansion of utility-scale battery capacity is associated with changes in the CAISO SP15 day-ahead evening-minus-midday electricity price spread. Using daily data from May 2023 through December 2025, the analysis highlights a key identification challenge: battery capacity and the underlying time trend move almost together.
